Whiteout 3.12.6 — Auto-close + wind

Changes from 3.12.5:
- AutoDoorPolicy.java, AutoClosingDoors.java: regular doors close 210 ticks after opening.
  ElectricDoors.isManaged() explicitly protects all Whiteout electricity-bound iron/copper doors.
  Living entities (players and NPCs) prevent closing. Redstone-powered doors are not forced shut.
  Door tracking checks only opened doors and a small neighbourhood around active players every 20 ticks.
- WindMovementPolicy.java, WindMovement.java: server-side movement speed modifier only outdoors
  during an active blizzard and only when walking against wind. Max penalty 8%; no knockback.
  No effect for Creative/Spectator, flying, water, vehicles or outside blizzard zones.
  New operator command: /whiteout atmosphere wind-movement <true|false>
