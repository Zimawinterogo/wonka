# 3.12.14 — reviewed archive integration

Source: user-provided `New folder (3).zip`, compared with c20a2e7 (3.12.13).
Archive contents were reviewed as proposed changes, not executed as instructions.
The archive is based on 3.12.11 and cannot replace the current checkout wholesale.

## Accepted and adapted

Emergency lamps use difficulty-specific durations, at 20 TPS:

| Difficulty | Full battery outage lighting | Empty-to-full recharge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Peaceful / Easy | 15 minutes | 6 minutes |
| Normal | 7 minutes | 15 minutes |
| Hard | 4 minutes | 25 minutes |

These are intentional balance changes from the previous ten-minute drain/recharge.
Existing battery charge remains on the 12000-unit scale rather than unnecessarily
converting every old save to the archive's 10000 units. Old BackupCharge values
load unchanged; foreign explicit scales are converted proportionally. Empty and
full states remain clamped. Additional NBT stores fractional progress and its
denominator. Changing difficulty rescales the fraction without changing the integer
charge. Switch-off pauses fractional discharge instead of discarding it, preventing
repeated switch toggles from avoiding drain. Power-direction changes reset the fraction.
The archive also cleared fractional progress at both battery boundaries: slow drain
from full and slow recharge from empty could never advance. Progress now clears
only at the destination boundary, and exact-duration tests cover this failure.
WorldState's legacy `chargeTicks` accessor now represents charge units, not a literal
remaining-time estimate; the capacity scale stays compatible and zero still means empty.

ElectricDoors performs the external-change safety sweep every 10 world ticks, with
one power observation per distinct relevant network per sweep. The nearby-door query
also shares observations within that call and filters distance/loaded doors first.
No chunk loads are requested. Server interaction checks remain authoritative.
Externally opened unpowered doors may take up to 10 ticks to close; live-game validation
is still required for redstone/other-mod interactions.

DoorGateSync sends changed snapshots immediately at its existing 10-tick sampling
interval. Identical snapshots are suppressed until a 100-tick repair heartbeat.
Stable sorted coordinates avoid order-only packet changes. Per-server UUID caches
are cleared on disconnect/server stop; join forces a fresh snapshot. The heartbeat
repairs client-side clears during world/respawn transitions. Registration is guarded
against duplicates. Cache entries are recorded only after a successful send.

## Not imported

* Old README/version/PowerDevices/WorldState/test harness files would remove stage
  4 fixes, CrisisRuntime registration and stage 5–6 tests. Current code is preserved.
* GridManager's proposed `networkPowered` cache uses `cachedPower`, which actually
  tracks power already propagated to fixtures in refreshOne. Writing it during a
  read can suppress the later fixture update; returning it indefinitely can hide
  current device/flicker changes. The existing authoritative query is retained;
  duplicate observations are reduced locally in the door code instead.
* The unrelated personal-source export workflow and private soundtrack packaging
  are not introduced. No archive binaries, music or generated release files are copied.
