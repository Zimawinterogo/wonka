# Whiteout 3.12.18 — lake chapter follow-up

Source: 3.12.17 on `feature/winter-crisis-foundation` (5a76c37).
No world or region changes. Existing 3.12.17 lake world stays compatible.

- Brick generator room triggers dialogue upon actually entering its interior:
  x=8354..8357, y=72..74, z=8432..8436. No fuse-panel click needed,
  no trigger through the wall. Stage 6 -> stage 8 is one-shot.
- Final double-pane shattering sound uses two positioned BLOCK_GLASS_BREAK
  layers at 8410 80 8383 and 8410 81 8383, volumes 8.0 and 7.0.
  It respects the Minecraft BLOCKS volume slider.
- All ten lake lines have separate IDs (`lake_01_arrival` through
  `lake_10_glass_finale`) and are visible in `/whiteout dialogues`.
  The standard Whiteout editor materializes an override in the original
  `config/whiteout/dialogues.json`; no independent dialogue store.
  Deleting an override returns that scene to its verbatim bundled default.
  Text, speaker and portrait follow the user's saved Whiteout script.
- Client ignores duplicate dialogue IDs for one chapter run; reset and
  disconnect clear only lake-owned queued dialogue, not unrelated scripts.
- No changes to previously approved four objective titles, timing,
  generator coordinates, reserve wiring, glass-block edits or map regions.
- `/whiteout lake reset` remains the supported replay command. It
  resets the client-side one-shot dialogue guard as well.
